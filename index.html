About & Credits

Patterning is an independent fan project by Seva Shaposhnikov, inspired by the strange, shifting world of CONTROL Resonant. It turns everyday objects into kaleidoscopic posters as a tribute to the game's surreal visual language.

Remedy Entertainment owns the CONTROL franchise, including its trademarks, logos, and game assets. This project is unaffiliated with Remedy Entertainment and has no official endorsement.
